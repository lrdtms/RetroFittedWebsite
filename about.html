About

The Story

Retrofitted was born from a simple belief: that the cars we drive should reflect the people we are. Founded on a passion for fitment, camber, and the art of stance, we exist at the intersection of old-school culture and modern craftsmanship.

Every build tells a story. Every stance is a statement. We are Retrofitted — and this is our culture.
